# VocalCat - Social Media Management for Creators

VocalCat is an open-source social media management tool built with C# and Blazor, powered by PostgreSQL (or sqlite!). Our goal is to empower creators by providing a seamless, AI-driven platform to manage and amplify their voices across multiple social media platforms. Whether you're a solo creator, a growing influencer, or a brand, VocalCat helps you streamline your content creation and posting process, all while maintaining authenticity.

## Key Features

- **Smart Content Repurposing**: Transform a single piece of content into Twitter threads, LinkedIn posts, video scripts, and more.
- **Content Goal-Oriented Messaging**: Tailor your content’s tone and structure to specific goals, such as building authority, increasing engagement, or growing your audience.
- **Authenticity-First AI**: Our AI adapts your content for various platforms while ensuring your authentic voice is maintained.
- **Multi-Platform Scheduling**: Post to Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and more with a single click.
- **Easy-to-Use Interface**: A simple, intuitive design that lets you focus on what matters most—creating content.
- **Data-Driven Insights**: Gain insights into your audience engagement and optimize your content strategy with performance analysis and trend detection.

## How It Works

1. **Create Your Content**: Start with any content—an article, video transcript, or social post.
2. **Select Your Goal**: Choose a content goal and style to optimize your post for maximum impact.
3. **AI Tailors Your Message**: VocalCat’s AI adapts your content for each platform, preserving your unique voice.
4. **Smart Scheduling & Posting**: Review, schedule, and post to multiple platforms effortlessly.
